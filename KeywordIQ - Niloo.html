What is a stability score?

Every keyword gets a score from 0 to 100 calculated from 24 months of Google Trends data. The formula is simple: Stability = 100 − (standard deviation ÷ mean × 100). A score above 60 means the keyword has consistent, predictable volume every month — safe to invest in. Below 60 means the volume spikes and crashes based on external events — use it opportunistically, not as a core budget item.

🪨 Stable Portfolio — score 60–100

Consistent demand month over month. Invest here with link building, deep content, and technical SEO. This is 70–80% of budget. Predictable ROI. The growth simulator in Part 3 models this curve.

Examples: "SEO Agentur Wien" (88) · "B2B Marketing" (79) · "Local SEO Wien" (76)
⚡ Volatile Watchlist — score 0–59

Spikes driven by news events, AI trends, regulation changes, or seasonal patterns. High upside if you act at the right moment. Fast content, no heavy link-building. 20–30% of budget. The alert system in Part 3 monitors these.

Examples: "ChatGPT für SEO" (38) · "Google Algorithm Update" (12) · "SEO Weihnachten" (42)

What the simulation shows — explained simply

The ML model runs two parallel projections for the stable keyword portfolio, both starting from the prospect's current traffic today. A third line shows the bonus traffic from acting on volatile keyword alerts at the right moment.

SEO Optimised (red line)

What happens if the agency works the stable keyword portfolio every month — link building, content, technical SEO. Modelled by a Random Forest trained on 120+ real client outcomes. This is the investment curve. This is what the client is paying for.

Organic Only (dashed line)

What happens if the client does nothing — no SEO, no agency. Domain drifts naturally upward at ~8–12% per year then plateaus. This is the cost of inaction. The gap between this and the red line is the value the agency delivers. Modelled from paused and churned clients.

Volatile Boost (blue line)

The extra traffic captured by publishing fast content when a volatile keyword spikes. This is optional and opportunistic — it comes on top of the stable portfolio investment. Example: when "ChatGPT für SEO" trended +340%, clients who published a guide that week captured 3–4× normal traffic for 8 weeks.
